## How to Use This File

After every real usage session, answer:
- What did I tap 3+ times?
- What took too long?
- What did I ignore completely?
- What was missing when I needed it?
- What felt risky to do while driving?
- What surprised me (good or bad)?

One paragraph is fine. A single sentence is fine. Just write it.
